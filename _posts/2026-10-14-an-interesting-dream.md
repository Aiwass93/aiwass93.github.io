I dreamed that I had to read aloud a poem from an ancient book. I’d printed it out on A4 paper, three pages in all.

As for what was on those pages, the first half of page one was just repetition of two different lines, most of page two was laughter, and page three was nothing but gibberish. I was pretty frustrated at the time because I had no idea how to pronounce a lot of it. On top of that, the print formatting had gone wrong and some lines were so long that they ran right over other text.

As I remember it, the poem was connected to Gnosticism, or maybe it was an excerpt from some Gnostic text. Later in the dream, I played Current 93’s Thunder Perfect Mind, because the mood felt like such a good fit (I had no idea yet that the two were connected).

When I woke up, I did a quick search and found that the fucking album really is Gnostic-themed. Its title comes from the Gnostic text The Thunder, Perfect Mind, and sure enough, it’s full of repetition and self-contradiction (“I am the first and the last, I am the honored one and the scorned one...”). I knew nothing about Gnosticism or the background of that album beforehand, and yet somehow I dreamed it all up. I can’t imagine what kind of thunderously perfect state of mind I must have been in that night.

I’m going to try to recreate the poem as faithfully as I can in the future. I have a feeling it would sound great read aloud.
